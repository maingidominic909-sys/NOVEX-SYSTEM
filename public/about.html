Our story

Supply is a promise. We keep it.

Institutions run on dependable supply — a school kitchen can't wait, a clinic can't improvise, a county programme can't pause. Norvex was built to make procurement boring in the best way: the right goods, at the agreed price, when we said they'd arrive.

From our desk in Upper Hill, Nairobi, we consolidate thirteen departments into one accountable supply chain — with volume pricing published openly, formal quotations issued fast, and every order tracked from receipt to delivery note.
